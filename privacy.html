Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to RelaxMind ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our iOS application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. The information we collect may include:

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous data about how you interact with the app, such as the features you use or the duration of your sessions, to improve the user experience.
  • Local Data: All your personal entries, mood logs, and progress are stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any usage data collected is used solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the RelaxMind app.
  • Understand user preferences to add relevant features and content.
  • Fix bugs and optimize app performance.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or have access to your payment information (such as credit card numbers). For more details, please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may use third-party analytics tools (such as Apple's default opt-in analytics) that collect anonymous, aggregated data to help us understand app usage.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously. Since your personal data (such as mood history and habits) is stored locally on your device or in your iCloud, its security is heavily reliant on your device's security settings (e.g., passcode, Face ID) and Apple's infrastructure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your primary data (such as mood logs and progress) is stored locally on your device or safely in your personal iCloud, you have complete control over it. You can completely delete all your data at any time simply by deleting the app from your device. If we collect any anonymous usage data, it is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You may also opt out of analytics through your device's privacy settings.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy or through an in-app notice.

8. Contact Us
